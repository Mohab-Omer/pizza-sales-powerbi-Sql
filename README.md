# pizza-sales-powerbi
## 🍕 Pizza Sales Analysis Dashboard | Power BI | SQL
An end-to-end Business Intelligence project built using Power BI to analyze pizza sales performance, customer purchasing behavior, and product profitability. The dashboard transforms raw transactional data into actionable insights that support data-driven business decisions.

## 📌 Project Overview
This project analyzes one year of pizza sales data (January–December 2015) to answer key business questions:
When do customers place the most orders?
Which pizzas generate the highest revenue?
Which products are underperforming?
How can the business improve staffing, inventory, and marketing strategies?
The dashboard provides a clear view of sales trends, customer demand patterns, and product performance.

## 🎯 Business Objectives
Analyze overall sales performance.
Identify peak sales periods.
Evaluate pizza category performance.
Determine best-selling and least-performing products.
Generate actionable business recommendations.

## 📈 Key Insights
# 📅 Sales Trends
Fridays and Saturdays are the busiest days.
January and July recorded the highest monthly sales.
Customer demand peaks during lunch and evening hours.
# 🍕 Category Performance
Classic Pizzas are the top-performing category.
Contributed 26.91% of total sales.
Generated 14,888 orders.
# 🏆 Best-Selling Pizza
Thai Chicken Pizza
Highest revenue-generating product.
Generated approximately $43K in revenue.
# 📉 Lowest-Performing Pizza
Brie Carre Pizza
Lowest revenue: $11.6K
Fewest orders: 480
